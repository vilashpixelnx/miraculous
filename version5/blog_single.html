10 Questions with Ava Cornish

By Emma ClarkeMar 18, 20266 min read3 comments

Ava Cornish is having a big year. Her debut album Walking Promises topped the charts in March, and her new single is already one of the most played songs on Miraculous. We met her in a small cafe in Camden to talk about writing, touring and the song that almost did not happen.

Where did the new single come from?

"I wrote it on a rainy night in Lisbon. I was sitting by the window of a tiny apartment, listening to the rain, and the melody just arrived. I recorded it on my phone so I wouldn't forget it."

"The best songs feel like they were already there. You just have to be quiet enough to hear them."

She almost left it off the record. "It felt too simple. My producer, Brian, kept playing it in the car until I gave in. Now it's the one people sing back at shows."

Three things on her tour rider

  • A kettle and a box of builder's tea
  • Her mother's old acoustic guitar
  • A notebook for new lyrics every night

Ava heads out on her European tour next month, with dates in London, Paris and Berlin. Her new album is expected later this year.
